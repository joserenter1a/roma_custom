— Our Legacy —

Built on Two Decades of Honest Work

Every joint, every reveal, every finish carries our name.

For more than twenty years, Roma Custom Specialties has designed and built custom woodwork for homes across Modesto and the Central Valley. We are a hands-on shop: the people who measure your space are the people who build and install the work.

Our approach is simple and old-fashioned in the best way. We choose solid materials, use joinery meant to last, and finish every piece as if it were going in our own home. The result is woodwork that feels like it has always belonged there.

“The Romans built things that are still standing two thousand years later. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.”

— Roberto Renteria, Founder, Roma Custom Specialties

Our Process

From the first conversation to the final coat of finish, there are four steps.

  1. I

    Consultation

    We visit your home, listen to how you live, measure the space, and talk through budget and materials.

  2. II

    Design

    Detailed drawings, wood and finish samples, and a clear, itemized quote, refined until it's right.

  3. III

    Craftsmanship

    Your pieces are built in our shop with careful joinery and hand-applied finishes.

  4. IV

    Installation

    Clean, careful installation and a final walkthrough. We leave the space better than we found it.
